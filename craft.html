Master Tailoring Philosophy

The 6-Stage Bespoke Journey

True bespoke is an art form. Unlike industrial ready-to-wear, every Big Boss & Co suit begins with 32 anatomical points, bespoke paper drafting, floating horsehair canvas, and multiple trial fittings.

C.G. Road Atelier Tradition

"A garment must mold to your bone structure, not constrain it."

For over three decades on C.G. Road, our master cutters have upheld the rigorous standards of classical Savile Row construction. We never use fused glue interlinings that bubble or degrade. Every jacket chest piece is hand-stitched with natural horsehair canvas that breathes and shapes permanently to your posture over time.

Step-by-Step Discipline

The Six Milestones of Creation

01

Sartorial Consultation

You meet directly with our senior styling advisor at our C.G. Road showroom. We review your occasion, venue lighting, personal posture, and drape preferences.

02

32 Anatomical Measurements

Beyond simple chest and waist dimensions, we measure shoulder pitch, spinal curvature, neck slope, arm rotation, and leg break to achieve a truly customized fit.

03

Paper Pattern Drafting

Our master cutter drafts a unique 2D blueprint of your body onto heavy card. This paper pattern is preserved in our physical archive for all future orders.

04

Skeleton Basting Trial

The raw fabric and floating horsehair canvas are temporarily assembled with white basting thread. You try on this prototype garment to sculpt shoulder lines and drape.

05

Hand-Finishing & Details

Over 1,000 hand stitches secure the canvas, roll the lapels, set the armholes, and stitch functional surgeon buttonholes, silk lining, and inner breast monogram.

06

Final Handover & Guarantee

Your finished masterpiece is presented in a breathable cedar-lined garment carrier, backed by our lifetime complimentary alteration and fit guarantee.
