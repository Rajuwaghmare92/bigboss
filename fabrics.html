Sartorial Science

Understanding Textile GSM & Seasonal Weaves

GSM (Grams per Square Meter) defines the density, warmth, and drape of suiting fabric.

210 - 240 GSM
Tropical Summer

Open-weave wools, pure Irish linen, and summer cottons. Perfect for daytime outdoor weddings in Gujarat.

250 - 280 GSM
All-Season Core

Super 140s Italian twill and worsted wool. Year-round comfort in air-conditioned boardrooms and banquet halls.

290 - 330 GSM
Structured Regal

Heavy raw silk brocades and textured jacquards. Provides authoritative structure to wedding sherwanis.

340+ GSM
Winter Luxury & Velvet

Flannels, cashmere overcoating, and midnight velvet dinner jackets for December/January gala occasions.
