Sartorial Discipline

The 5-Step Bespoke Journey

Unlike machine-made ready-to-wear, each garment at Big Boss & Co is drafted individually around your unique body contours.

01

Consultation & Silhouette

We discuss your occasion, comfort requirements, posture habits, and select your ideal lapel, vents, and cut.

02

Fabric & Lining Curation

Touch and feel hundreds of fabric rolls in our showroom aisle, matching lining jacquards and button accents.

03

28-Point Anatomical Measure

Our master cutter records precision metrics including chest drop, shoulder slope, arm rotation, and waist balance.

04

Hand-Basted Skeleton Trial

You try on the unfinished basted garment to fine-tune collar hugs, sleeve pitch, and waistband comfort.

05

Final Hand Press & Delivery

Hand-stitched pick lapels, finished buttonholes, final steam pressing, and delivery in our protective suiting bag.

100% Artisan Hand-Drafted • 0% Pre-Cut
Pure Artisanal Philosophy

No Pre-Cut Blocks. No Factory Assembly.

Every suit jacket, sherwani, and bandi at Big Boss & Co is hand-drawn directly onto your cloth using tailor’s chalk and sheared by our master artisan. This honors the true Savile Row tradition where the textile grain matches your individual posture and movement.

✓
Anatomical Pitch Compensation

Accounts for erect or stooped postures, dropped shoulders, and arm rotation.

✓
Floating Horsehair Canvas Basting

Molds naturally to your chest over time without rigid glue fusing.

✓
Hand-Finished Pick Stitching

Silk thread pick-stitching along lapels, pockets, and working surgeon cuffs.

Tailor's Precision Guide

Why Bespoke Fit Matters

A ready-made suit is crafted for a generic average. A Big Boss bespoke cut accounts for your natural posture, neck pitch, and shoulder balance.

1
Collar Hug (No Gap)

The jacket collar rests flush against your shirt collar with zero buckling or gaping.

2
Shoulder Pitch & Seam

Seams end exactly at your shoulder bone with soft roped or natural unpadded sleeveheads.

3
Sleeve Length & Shirt Cuff

Engineered to reveal exactly 1/2 inch of crisp shirt cuff when arms hang naturally.

4
Clean Trouser Break

Tailored with a half or slight break over your shoes for elongated, clean silhouette lines.
